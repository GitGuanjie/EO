# Next research mandate: symmetry-cycled SOEO–EO detectors → a useful fault-aware QEC module

Read `MAIN_REPORT_EN.md`, the eight exact 13P grid CSVs and the 12-round per-cycle reset schedule. Re-run `code/full_spin_independent.py`, `code/reproduce.py`, and paired-noise experiments in `code/simulate.py`. Baseline is the exact 13P 7-spin SOEO S=0 detector, NOT a high-level XXXX/ZZZZ measurement. Treat 4-round V4 cycling as numerically demonstrated under the stated model; 12-round mixed forward/inverse is a proof of mechanism with considerable resource overhead. Do not claim exact 1st-order physical gate cancellation, fault tolerance or a QEC threshold.

1. **Instrument and physical-fault audit:** For all 8 native routed 13P variants, compute complete conditional Kraus maps and all single-physical-spin and single-physical-exchange-pulse faults at every time location, including J89 routing, ancilla singlet preparation, imperfect ST readout and reset. Distinguish data S=1/S=2 spin-sector faults, silent encoded logical errors, gauge effects and higher-level syndrome changes. Quantify minimum (not only average) single-fault detection with optimal V4 mixtures. Test both static gain and IID noise as well as Overhauser/field gradients and context-dependent gain.

2. **Short coherence-cancellation search, not padding:** Build symmetry-cycled **4-round or shorter** 13P-or-less instruments whose total accepted-logical first-order gain Gram vanishes (or becomes <0.01) for every persistent physical bond. Jointly optimize different exactly nominal detector angle families, positive-area inverses, V4 patterns and adaptive schedules. Because the baseline 11P nominal constraint Jacobian has rank 10 of 11 in the local 5D spin0 representation, use 1D manifold continuation rather than blind 11D optimization. Verify all hard constraints to <1e-10; no acceptance of nominal error >1e-8. Report real pulse area and number of readouts, not just raw pulse count. A preliminary 12-round 8F/4I symmetric word has `H_accepted=0.02855` but not zero, and at σ=.015 higher orders dominate; aim to reduce the **finite σ** conditional error and measurement resource simultaneously.

3. **Noise correlation decision:** The V4 sequence improves dramatically for temporally persistent gain but can lose at rho=0. Compare rho = 0,0.5,0.9,1 at matched σ=.015, σiid=.0006 and realistic readout. Implement model selection/inference for persistent gain from prior syndrome streams, and compare a controller choosing fixed/K4/mixed schedules with explicit calibration overhead. Do not randomly twirl blindly if gain is IID dominated.

4. **Large-architecture EO↔SOEO integration:** Recover the chronological 47P/25D R144 physical 9-spin `Z_A Z_C` parity CSV; verify both Kraus branches, full logical×gauge and any leakage in 11 spins when each data EO is promoted to an SOEO with a spectator fourth spin. Propose a graph where local spin-sector checks and high-level EO ancilla checks coexist with no unintended J-path or gauge coupling. Then build actual `XXXX/ZZZZ` parity measurement on 4-SOEO data and one EO ancilla, with physical routing, all controls Heisenberg exchange only. Do NOT substitute an ideal 116P abstract circuit for a native 19-spin sequence.

5. **Fair QEC-level benchmark:** Under identical hardware time, compare no local detector, the existing 13P fixed detector, V4 4-round cycling, and HRL-style EO LRU/RIL as available. Include singlet preparation, real non-projective ST readout, reset, ancilla crosstalk, shuttling and correlated-gain persistence across high-level QEC rounds. Report attempts, acceptance, throughput per unit time, erasure flags, leakage, hook rates, and *decoded logical failure per code cycle* for explicit codes. No claims based solely on postselected fidelity. Prioritize tractable d=3 code before d>3.

6. **Literature and originality:** Compare rigorously against Sun et al., PRX Quantum 5, 020356 (2024), full-permutation DD; Heinz/Sharma/Kattemölle arXiv:2608.14214; and exchange-only dynamically corrected gates, SAGE/SOEO architectures. Show exactly what the new group-twirled syndrome instrument adds beyond known permutation DD and whether this survives nonideal measurement.

Deliver independently validated chronological CSV, executable code with random seeds, first-order physical and accepted-logical Gram matrices, circuit-level fault path table, memory-limited search settings, a realistic Pareto set, and a publish/no-publish decision. Do not spend most of the budget simply appending more pulses to reduce an idealized harm score.
